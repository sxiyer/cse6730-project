This file records high-level notes of the scalper logic.

# The Naive Gambler
**Strategy**: Buys and Sells cards randomly regardless of price or demand.
No intelligence at all.
Use him as a baseline.

# The Shadow
**Strategy**: If the price of a card is going up, Buy first and Sell later. 
If the price of a card is going down, do the opposite.

# The Market Maker
**Strategy**: Buys slightly below current price, Sells slightly above current price.
Basically a fixed spread idea.
He takes advantage of small oscillations to make a profit.

# The Quick Grabber
**Strategy**: Enter when price of a card moves quickly in one direction, then quickly exit once a certain profit is reached.
He rides micro-momentum to make a profit.
